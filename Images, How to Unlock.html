Grave Matters is a Halloween-themed VALORANT capsule featuring a cute ghost called Shriek, with an animated gun buddy, player card and spray, but no weapon skins. The gun buddy pulls out a tiny knife when you equip your melee weapon and becomes a tombstone when you die. The items are expected to be sold together, with a release date of 27 October 2026 in the Americas and 28 October elsewhere. The bundle price will be 1,375 VP.
