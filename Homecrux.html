BLUETTI Apex 200 Portable Power Station Early Launch Offer Starts at $1,099

BLUETTI listens to its customer feedback, and the proof comes in the form of the new Apex 200, which caters to the portability issues people have highlighted in early reviews of the Apex 300. This portable power station drops 11 kg (24 lbs) of weight from its predecessor, making it suitable for home backup as well as RV travel. Weighing 27 kg (59.5 lbs), it is 34 percent smaller by volume and can be lifted by a single person into a vehicle or carried around a campsite, according to the company.

Despite the decreased size, the Apex 200 retains high solar capabilities and modular expansion properties of the Apex 300. Built in the same ecosystem, it combines 2,560Wh of LFP capacity with a 3.2kW–3.6kW standalone AC output. You can connect three Apex 200 units via HUB A1 to increase the combined storage capacity to 100kWh for whole-home backup, along with increased AC power output of 6,000W. It can also be equipped with up to six B500K expansion batteries to increase the original 2.56kWh capacity to 33.28kWh.

BLUETTI says adding one or two B300K batteries can extend essential home backup to 2-3 days. For extended outages, you can use up to 3,000W of solar input to replenish the system. With SORA 500 portable solar panels, the system tops up the battery from 0 to 80 percent in just 50 minutes and achieves a full 100 percent charge in 1.7 hours using solar energy. You can also charge it via wall AC, gas generators, and vehicle charging to support indoor and outdoor adventures.

The Apex 200 features a built-in TT-30R port for travel trailers and a high-draw 12V/30A Anderson port to run DC appliances directly without conversion losses. There are four independently controlled smart AC outlets that can be switched using the BLUETTI app. Users can remotely switch individual outlets, set automated operation schedules, apply state-of-charge controls, and preserve stored energy for priority loads during extended blackouts.

The system also provides 15ms UPS switching for sensitive electronics and integrates seamlessly with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant ecosystems. The Apex series is modular, meaning you can stack extra batteries onto it later. The Apex 200 offers a more accessible entry point designed to start small and scale as power needs evolve.

BLUETTI designed the Apex 200 as a more affordable starting point so users don’t have to make high investment to get high-voltage solar setup. If you wish to buy, early pricing is available across launch packages from November 4 through November 18, with the standalone Apex 200 portable power station starting at $1,099.

The BLUETTI Apex 200 portable power station officially launches on November 4, offering its lowest standalone price of $1,099. After the initial launch window, standalone prices will gradually increase until December 5, while bundle prices will remain unchanged.
