Flambe merge games have their official facebook page where you can get a chance to gain a large amount of energy by commenting on their post. If you have extra luck then you can get more energy by mentioning your game ID in the comment box with answer ask in the post.

In the Flambe merge you need to upgrade your restaurant to complete the tasks, after completing all the tasks your restaurant will be fully upgraded and you get free energy rewards.
