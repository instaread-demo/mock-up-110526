Cirque du Soleil thinks its best critics might be kids

by Vicki Duong on November 4th, 2026 at 1:36 PM
1 of 1 2 of 1
  • Anne Colliard

Maybe the best person to review a show is someone who hasn’t learned to pretend they’re not amazed as people flip through the air. For the kid who’s dreamed of running off to join the circus, Cirque du Soleil has another opening, this time from a seat in the audience as a junior critic.

The company is looking for Vancouver kids under 12 to take in LUZIA beneath the Big Top. Selected junior critics and their families will get tickets and snacks for a performance during the week of November 16. Afterwards, the kids will share their honest reviews in a short video interview and written piece, from the gravity-defying acts to the parts that made them giggle.

A surreal trip through an imaginary Mexico, LUZIA follows a traveller through scenes that shift from an old movie set to an ocean, a dance hall, and an arid desert. Its characters and imagery draw on the country’s landscapes, traditions, and mythology. Acrobats take the form of hummingbirds, complete with wings and long beaks, while water becomes part of the performance. It’s the first Cirque du Soleil touring production to incorporate water into its acrobatics, which should give young reviewers plenty to talk about beyond the flips.

The reviews are expected to appear on Cirque du Soleil’s social channels and website the week of November 23, with the possibility of being featured by select media outlets. Honest opinions are encouraged; “I liked the snacks best” may also be a valid review.

Parents and guardians can apply on behalf of children under 12 by filling out a Google Form by November 8. Applications can be written or submitted as a short video explaining why their child has what it takes to be one of Vancouver’s biggest little reviewers.

LUZIA opens tomorrow (November 5) at Concord Pacific Place. Apply here.
