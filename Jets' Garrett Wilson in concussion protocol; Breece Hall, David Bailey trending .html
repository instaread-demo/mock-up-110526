Jets' Garrett Wilson in concussion protocol; Breece Hall, David Bailey trending in right direction

Hall appears to be closing in on his potential return

11/4/2026, 12:25 PM

John Flanigan

Freelance Editorial Producer

The Jets received some good and bad news ahead of Wednesday's practice.

Head coach Aaron Glenn said that star WR Garrett Wilson is in the concussion protocol. 

Glenn didn't reveal exactly how Wilson suffered the head injury, but said that he came in Monday with the issue, and is being viewed as day-to-day as he works his way through the protocol.

Wilson still has time to clear through the steps, but if he were to miss Sunday's game against the Chiefs, Adonai Mitchell and Omar Cooper Jr. would need to step up for Gang Green's offense. 

The Jets could also be receiving some help in the run and pass game, though, as star running back Breece Hall is trending in the right direction toward his return from a quad injury. 

Hall has been progressing over the past couple of weeks, but the Jets have opted to play it safe. 

Backup Braelon Allen has slid tremendously into the top spot in his place, but having the two of them work as a tandem out of the backfield could be a big boost, especially if Wilson ends up sidelined. 

Additionally, rookie pass rusher David Bailey is trending positively after missing last game with a groin injury.
