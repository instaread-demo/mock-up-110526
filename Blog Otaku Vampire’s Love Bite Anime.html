Otaku Vampire’s Love Bite Anime

Feast on blood, gorge on the anime!

Some vampires are vegetarian, some vampires indulge in o-negative, but soon you can gorge on the new Otaku Vampire’s Love Bite anime.

The latest hit series from the creator of Kamisama Kiss, Julietta Suzuki, is getting an anime adaptation! Feast your eyes on the announcement trailer below and prepare to take a bite out of love.

More about Otaku Vampire’s Love Bite:

Even vampires obsess over their favorite characters!

After spending 30 years as a shut-in, the vampire Hina moves to Japan to enjoy otaku activities. There, she meets her neighbor, Kyuta Amanatsu, a boy who looks just like her favorite character! What’s more, his blood is something special, irresistible to vampires everywhere.

As other vampires show up with their sights set on Kyuta, his relationship with Hina takes an unexpected turn.
Is she fangirling…or falling in love?!

A 100% otaku vampire meets a handsome, slightly sharp-tongued guy in this bloody romantic comedy!

Craving the day Hina’s otaku dreams play out on screen? Satiate your hunger with manga!
