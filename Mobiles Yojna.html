Introduction:

The Realme 16 5G is a feature-packed mid-range smartphone that focuses on a premium AMOLED display, a large battery, a slim design, and a high-resolution selfie camera. Launched in India in 2026, the phone combines the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 Turbo chipset with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, making it suitable for everyday use, entertainment, photography, and moderate gaming.

One of the biggest highlights of the Realme 16 5G is its 6.57-inch flexible AMOLED display. The panel offers FHD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, 10-bit colour depth, and up to 4,200 nits peak brightness. This makes the phone particularly attractive for users who prioritize display quality for watching videos, browsing, and gaming.

Performance is handled by the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 Turbo, paired with LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 storage. The phone is available with 8GB or 12GB of RAM, while storage options include 128GB and 256GB. A dedicated memory-card slot is also supported, giving users the option to expand storage.

Photography is another important part of the Realme 16 5G experience. The rear camera system consists of a 50MP primary camera and a 2MP monochrome sensor, while the front features a high-resolution 50MP selfie camera. The front camera can also record 1080p video at up to 60fps.

The smartphone packs a large 7,000mAh battery with 60W fast charging. Realme has also equipped it with features such as an in-display fingerprint sensor, infrared remote control, gyroscope, and dual-SIM 5G connectivity.

Design and Build Quality

The Realme 16 5G features a modern flat design with a pill-shaped rear camera module. The phone uses a predominantly polycarbonate construction but maintains a relatively premium appearance and is surprisingly light for a device carrying a 7,000mAh battery.

At around 183 grams and 8.1mm thick, the Realme 16 5G is considerably easier to handle than many other large-battery smartphones.

The phone is offered in Air Black and Air White. Its IP69 rating provides strong protection against dust and water, adding another layer of durability for everyday use.

Display

The 6.57-inch AMOLED display is one of the strongest features of the Realme 16 5G. It offers 2372 × 1080 FHD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, 10-bit colour depth, and 397 PPI pixel density.

The panel supports 100% DCI-P3 coverage in supported display modes and can reach up to 4,200 nits peak brightness. Realme lists 1,000 nits typical brightness and 1,400 nits HBM brightness.

The 120Hz refresh rate makes scrolling and animations feel smooth, while the AMOLED panel delivers deeper blacks and stronger contrast than a conventional LCD. For Netflix, YouTube, social media, and general multimedia use, the display is a major advantage.

Performance

The Realme 16 5G is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 Turbo, an octa-core 5G chipset paired with Mali-G57 MC2 graphics. RAM configurations include 8GB and 12GB LPDDR4X, while storage is available in 128GB and 256GB UFS 2.2 versions.

For everyday activities such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Chrome, YouTube, multitasking, and streaming, the phone delivers a responsive experience.

However, gaming performance is not its strongest area. Independent testing has found the phone’s gaming performance to be underwhelming for demanding graphics-heavy titles, despite its cooling system.

For casual and moderate gaming, the Realme 16 5G should be adequate, but users seeking maximum gaming performance at this price should consider phones with more powerful processors.

Camera

The Realme 16 5G has a dual rear-camera setup consisting of a 50MP primary camera and a 2MP monochrome sensor. The main camera uses an f/1.8 aperture and autofocus.

Realme provides several shooting modes, including Photo, Video, Portrait, Night, Panorama, Cinematic, Slow Motion, Dual-View Video, Time-Lapse, Hi-Res, Google Lens, Underwater, Tilt-Shift, Food, and AI Landscape.

50MP Main Camera

The 50MP primary camera is designed for everyday photography and provides the highest level of detail available from the rear camera system.

In good lighting conditions, it can produce usable photographs, but independent testing has criticized the overall primary-camera performance, particularly in situations where better image processing and dynamic-range handling are expected.

2MP Monochrome Camera

The secondary 2MP monochrome camera primarily contributes to portrait and imaging effects. It is not a replacement for a dedicated ultrawide or telephoto camera.

This means the Realme 16 5G offers less camera versatility than some competitors that include ultrawide or telephoto lenses.

50MP Selfie Camera

The 50MP front camera is one of the phone’s standout photography specifications. It uses an f/2.4 aperture and an 86-degree field of view.

The selfie camera supports Portrait, Night, Panorama, Dual-View Video, Time-Lapse, Retouch, Screen Fill Light, Hi-Res, and Underwater modes.

For social media, video calls, and selfies, the high-resolution front camera gives the Realme 16 5G an advantage over many conventional mid-range phones.

Video Recording

The rear camera can record video at 1080p 60fps and 30fps, with 720p recording also available. Cinematic video, dual-view recording, time-lapse, digital zoom, and 720p slow-motion at 120fps are supported.

The front camera can also record 1080p video at up to 60fps, making it useful for vloggers and social-media creators who prefer recording directly from the selfie camera.

One notable limitation is the lack of 4K video recording.

Battery Life

The Realme 16 5G packs a 7,000mAh battery, making battery endurance one of its strongest features. Realme lists a 6,830mAh rated capacity and 7,000mAh typical capacity.

The phone supports 60W fast charging, along with PPS 45W, PD 2.0 at 13.5W, and BC1.2 at 10W charging standards.

With such a large battery, the phone is well suited to users who spend long hours watching videos, browsing social media, using navigation, or gaming. Independent testing also found that the battery can comfortably last beyond a day of heavy use.

Gaming Features

The Realme 16 5G combines the Dimensity 6400 Turbo chipset with a 120Hz AMOLED display and a large 7,000mAh battery.

The high-refresh-rate screen helps make supported games feel smoother, while the large battery allows longer gaming sessions without constantly looking for a charger.

However, the processor is not aimed at flagship-level gaming. Independent testing found that demanding games can struggle to maintain smooth performance, so the Realme 16 5G is better suited to casual and moderate gaming rather than serious competitive gaming.

Software and Features

The Realme 16 5G runs Android 16 with realme UI 7.0. The software includes a wide range of AI-powered tools and customization options.

The phone includes an in-display optical fingerprint sensor and facial recognition. Other sensors include an accelerometer, gyroscope, electronic compass, proximity sensor, ambient-light sensor, colour-temperature sensor, and infrared remote control.

Connectivity features include dual Nano-SIM support, 5G, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth, GPS, USB Type-C, and OTG. NFC and eSIM are not supported according to the official specifications.

Should You Buy the Realme 16 5G?

The Realme 16 5G is a good option for buyers who want a combination of a high-quality AMOLED display, large battery, lightweight design, high-resolution selfie camera, and durable construction.

Its 6.57-inch 120Hz AMOLED panel, 7,000mAh battery, 60W charging, IP69 protection, and 50MP selfie camera make it particularly attractive for multimedia users, social-media users, and people who prioritize battery life.

The main weaknesses are its gaming performance and rear-camera versatility. The Dimensity 6400 Turbo is adequate for everyday tasks but is not the best choice for demanding gaming, while the absence of an ultrawide camera limits photography flexibility.

Overall, the Realme 16 5G is a strong battery-and-display-focused smartphone. If your priorities are AMOLED quality, long battery life, selfies, durability, and everyday performance, it is worth considering. If gaming performance and advanced rear-camera capabilities are your top priorities, there are better alternatives in the same price range.
