These Christmas wood slice ornaments turn vintage paper napkins into charming holiday keepsakes. With a little ivory paint, Mod Podge, and your favorite Christmas designs, you can create nostalgic tree decorations or handmade gifts. Natural bark edges and burgundy velvet ribbon give each ornament a warm, rustic finish—perfect for classic Santas, snowy cottages, festive birds, and old-fashioned Christmas trees.

DIY Vintage Christmas Ornaments: How to Decoupage Wood Slices

1. Gather Your Supplies

Set everything out before you begin so you can place and smooth the napkin as soon as the glue is ready.

Choose napkin motifs that fit comfortably on the wood slices. Before cutting, hold a slice over the design to check where the main details will sit. You can also use decoupage rice paper or print your favorite motifs on printer-compatible rice paper—just test the ink for smearing first.

2. Prep the Wood

Lightly sand the flat face of each wood slice, leaving the bark edges natural. Wipe away all sanding dust with a clean cloth before painting.

3. Paint a Light Base

Brush a thin coat of Apple Barrel Ivory Acrylic Craft Paint onto the flat surface. The light background helps the napkin colors stand out. Let the paint dry completely.

4. Separate the Napkin Layers

Cut your chosen vintage motif slightly larger than the wood slice. Carefully peel away all plain backing layers, keeping only the thin printed top layer.

5. Brush on Decoupage Glue

Apply a thin, even coat of Mod Podge Matte to the painted surface. Cover the entire face, including near the edges, without leaving puddles.

6. Place and Gently Smooth

Center the printed napkin layer over the wet glue. Cover it with a loose piece of plastic wrap and gently smooth from the center outward. Use light pressure to avoid tearing the tissue, then carefully lift away the wrap.

7. Dry, Then Tidy the Edges

Let the ornament dry completely. Using fine-grit sandpaper, gently sand downward along the outer edge to remove excess paper. Avoid sanding across the printed design, and remove any loose dust.

8. Seal the Design

With a soft brush, gently apply a thin topcoat of Mod Podge Matte over the dry design. Avoid repeatedly brushing the same spot. Let the finish dry and cure according to the product label.

9. Add Ribbon and Hang

Carefully clear any paper or dried finish covering the predrilled hole. Thread burgundy velvet ribbon or natural twine through it and tie a secure hanging loop. Your ornament is ready for the Christmas tree or a gift box!

Tips for Beautiful Christmas Wood Slice Ornaments

  • Check the placement first. Keep faces and important details away from the hanging hole and outer edge.
  • Work with thin coats. Heavy layers of paint or glue can make the surface harder to work with.
  • Handle the tissue gently. A few small wrinkles add character; too much smoothing can tear the paper.
  • Allow time for curing. Follow the product label before wrapping or stacking finished ornaments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to paint the wood slices before decoupaging?

A light base is recommended for this look. The thin napkin layer allows the background to show through, so ivory paint helps the colors and details stand out.

Do I use all the layers of the paper napkin?

No. Peel away every plain backing layer and use only the printed top layer. Check the corners carefully, as thin backing layers can be easy to miss.

How do I keep the napkin from wrinkling?

Use a thin, even coat of glue and gently smooth through plastic wrap from the center outward. Avoid heavy pressure or repeatedly working the same area. A little texture is normal. 👉 For more tips, follow our guide on how to decoupage with napkins without wrinkles or tears.

Can I use rice paper instead of paper napkins?

Yes—choose rice paper intended for decoupage. For home-printed motifs, use printer-compatible paper and test that the ink won’t smear when you apply Mod Podge.

Conclusion

These Christmas wood slice ornaments are a simple way to turn vintage napkins into beautiful handmade keepsakes. The combination of natural wood, nostalgic holiday artwork, and soft velvet ribbon creates a timeless decoration that looks charming on any Christmas tree. Whether you’re crafting a matching ornament set for your home or making thoughtful handmade gifts, this easy decoupage project is one you’ll look forward to recreating year after year.
